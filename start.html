Shop CRM

Your leads, follow-ups, texts, forms and signed PDFs in one app on your phone. Solar, plumbing and HVAC.

Open the app

1. Put it on your Home Screen

iPhone
  1. Open this link in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button (the square with the arrow).
  3. Tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  4. Open it from the new icon from now on.
Android
  1. Open this link in Chrome.
  2. Tap the three dots menu.
  3. Tap Add to Home screen or Install app.

2. Set up your business (about 2 minutes)

  1. Pick your trades: Solar, Plumbing, HVAC, or more than one.
  2. Type your business name, add your logo and pick a color. Each trade can have its own.
  3. Backup: tap Back it up for me and type your email so your leads are saved off the phone. You can also skip and keep it on the phone only.
  4. A short tour is next. You can Skip the tour, or tap Try a form first to practice on Practice Customer before a real job.

3. What you can do

  • Today shows who to call or text next, so nobody slips.
  • + adds a lead or starts a form: estimate, invoice, sign-off, warranty and more.
  • Customers sign with a finger and get a clean PDF with your logo.
  • Text opens your own Messages app with the text already written. Nothing sends unless you send it.
  • Pipeline shows every job by stage, with a calendar.
  • More is where you change your look, your forms and your team.
